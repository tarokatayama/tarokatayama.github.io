Fantasy Football Draft Helper

A tool to help you prep for fantasy football drafts by hunting for value. ESPN's average draft position (ADP) reflects a lot of everyday, casual managers, so I compare it against the ADP from sharper platforms like Underdog, where people play for higher buy-ins and take it more seriously. I also fold in rankings from professionals like Hayden Winks to find where ADP and true value disagree, then plot those gaps so the best draft-day targets stand out.
